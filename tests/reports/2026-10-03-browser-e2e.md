## What the browser actually verified

- A landlord creates a draft and invitation; the tenant joins, accepts full terms, approves exactly 1,000 MockUSD, then separately funds it. Approval is asserted to leave funding zero.
- The browser submits early checkout with a fictional private document, agrees to checkout, submits cleaning 100/damage 200 and accepts/disputes items. Until claimDeadline no credit is allocated; after closing claims the page and contract show 700/100/200. 700 and 100 reach the respective wallets, leaving 200 unallocated.
- Primary reasons support landlord 50 / tenant 150 of disputed 200. Proposal does not release funds before challenge maturity. Public finalization gives total 850/150; both wallets withdraw and escrow token balance becomes zero.
- Primary and fallback timeouts remain separate steps, followed by exit notice and final 900/100 withdrawal.
- Modal cancel and rejected signature preserve nonce, accounting and funding. A fresh browser context reauthenticates the same external test wallet; unrelated wallet cannot load private balances. Repeated withdrawal cannot pay twice.
- The last scenario stops the Next UI, HTTP API and database. No Worker or sponsor service is running. An independent client derives the original test account, a third party transfers gas and calls public expiry/withdrawFor. Tokens go only to the original tenant, never the payer.

Per-scenario JSON retains actual receipt status, block hash/number, sender/target, amounts and source commit/dirty-tree metadata. Generated balance screenshots and detailed logs/results are CI artifacts under `test-results/e2e-local/`. The checked-in runner JSON is [e2e-2026-10-03.json](e2e-2026-10-03.json); hashes there belong solely to disposable local chains and cannot be looked up on a public explorer.
